Automatic Review Requests

When a job is marked done, your customer automatically gets this. No chasing, no forgetting.

1. Text, 2 hours after the job

2. Email, same time

3. Follow-up, 3 days later (only if no review yet)

"g.page/r/your-business/review" stands in for your real Google review link. In a real setup, messages go out from your business email (texts need a low-cost SMS account, a few cents per message), and customers who already reviewed are skipped.
