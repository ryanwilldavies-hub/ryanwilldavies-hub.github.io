Dr. Chu’s point

$18/hour is the wrong fight.

A virtual MOA at $18/hour is daytime staffing. At that rate you can hire a person — and a person should still answer during clinic hours. Offshore AI mixed into a live medical line is a privacy and quality problem, not a bargain.

Nights and weekends are different math. Abbotsford closes at 4pm. Surrey at 5pm. Coquitlam at 5:30. Saturday and Sunday nobody is there. Staffing those hours at $18 is three shifts, not one. People who call about laser vision correction after dinner hang up. They do not leave a message. They call the next clinic on the list.

What the bot actually does

  • Answers on the first ring. Talk or type — same workflow.
  • Hours and addresses for Abbotsford, Surrey, Chilliwack, Coquitlam.
  • LASIK / PRK / ICL: not MSP. Capture the consult. No diagnosis, no fee quote.
  • Cataract: typical MSP vs uninsured premium lens. No surgery booked on this line.
  • Existing patient: morning note. No clinical advice, no reschedule after hours.
  • Red eye / sudden vision loss / injury → ER or 911. Logged, not a callback.
  • Mandarin: flag for a morning callback. Does not fake clinical Mandarin.
  • Both nights/weekends and overflow. Not a $10/hour MOA, and not Kylie’s virtual-MOA hire.

The trial

Both. One line. Not a cheaper MOA.

Keep your people on the phones while you are open. This covers the two times they cannot: after close, and when every line is already lit. Flat monthly — not $10/hour, not $18/hour.

Nights and weekends

2 · Overflow

Lunch and peak

Front desk is with a patient. Three lines ringing. This picks up so the caller is not on hold, then dumped to voicemail. Staff still own the clinic hours.

One recovered laser consult

Uninsured laser vision correction is paid by the patient. One consult that becomes surgery covers months of after-hours answering. Daytime $18/hour does not.

Voicemail is a dead end

Most first-time callers who hit voicemail never leave a message and never call back. Answering on the first ring, then a morning list, beats “please leave your name and number.”

Trial one line first

Forward Abbotsford or Surrey after close, and overflow during open hours on that same line. If the morning list is empty after two weeks, you learned it. If it is not, you kept the lead.

If this goes live

Yes — it can sit on the clinic phone. After close (and on overflow), Abbotsford or Surrey forwards to a real number. That line answers with this script, takes the name and callback, sends true emergencies to ER, and puts a list on staff phones before 7:30am — email or text to the office, not a webpage. No diagnosis. No booking of MSP-insured surgery. Uninsured laser consults labelled as not MSP.

That needs a Fraser Valley number, call-forward, a script Dr. Chu signs as responsible licensee, and a PIPA review before any patient phone number sits on a server. This page is the workflow. The call above is a simulation — no live clinic phone is connected yet. Back to the note for Dr. Chu.
