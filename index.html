The practice

Ophthalmology in the Fraser Valley.

Co-founded in 2013 by Dr. Gabriel Chu and Dr. Joseph King, Fraser Valley Cataract & Laser is a comprehensive ophthalmology practice with a surgical facility in Surrey.

  • Founding surgeons had laser vision correction

    Dr. King chose LASIK for himself. Dr. Chu had laser vision correction with Dr. King in 2015. That is their personal history, not a promise of your result.

  • MSP and uninsured, labelled

    Medically necessary exams and standard cataract surgery are typically billed to MSP. LASIK, ICL, premium lenses, and cosmetic eyelid surgery are not.

  • Cataract surgery is usually a short procedure

    Typical time in the chair is minutes; the visit is longer. You are usually awake. Times and recovery vary. Risks are discussed at consult.

  • Four clinics

    Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Coquitlam, and Surrey.
