Dr. Austin Toresdahl

OD FAAO

Glaucoma, ocular surface, and specialty contact lenses

Optometrist. Licensed by the College of Health and Care Professionals of British Columbia.

Dr. Austin Toresdahl provides medical and surgical co-management. He earned an Honours BSc in Chemistry from the University of Alberta and a Doctor of Optometry from the University of Waterloo, then completed clinical training in Calgary and Grande Prairie.

He practised at an ophthalmology clinic in Chicago with subspecialists in cornea, retina, glaucoma, uveitis, oculoplastics, and pediatrics. Clinical interests include glaucoma, ocular surface and dry eye disease, and specialty contact lenses including sclerals. He is a Fellow of the American Academy of Optometry.
